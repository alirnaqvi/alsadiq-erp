Today’s meeting — a live look at the software

How we will record money, donors and majalis

آج ہم یہ سافٹ ویئر آپ کو چلا کر دکھائیں گے

This is not a paper plan. After these slides we will open the real system on the computer and walk through it together.

In simple words

What this software is for

یہ سافٹ ویئر کیا کام کرتا ہے؟

It takes the work that used to live in registers, Excel files and memory, and puts it in one place that the office and the trustees can all see.

  • When someone gives money, a proper receipt is made at once
  • Every donor has one record — name, phone, membership number, city, and how much they have given
  • Upcoming majalis appear on a calendar, with both English and Islamic dates
  • A donor can sit at home, on a phone or a computer, and still see their own giving

Two kinds of people use it

Office staff, and donors

دفتر والے، اور معطین

دفتری استعمال

Office and volunteers

They sign in at the desk. They record money, print receipts, look after the donor list, approve a majlis sponsorship, and send WhatsApp messages.

معطی کا استعمال

Registered donors

They sign in with their mobile number. They see their receipts, send a payment, look at the calendar, and offer to sponsor a gathering.

Office — taking money

How the counter records a donation

کاؤنٹر پر عطیہ کیسے لکھا جاتا ہے

  1. Open Collect (وصولی).
  2. Type the donor’s mobile number. Their name appears. If they are new, type the name once.
  3. Choose what the money is for: Monthly Membership, or Others, or both.
  4. If you choose Others, a list opens. Pick one head, or more than one, and write the amount on each.
  5. Tick the box if this person is a monthly member.
  6. Save. A receipt number is given. Print it from the top of the next screen.

Office — the donor list

Every donor in one register

ہر معطی کا مکمل ریکارڈ

On the Donors screen you can search by name, phone or membership number. For each person we keep:

  • Name and contact number
  • Membership number (the old AS-… number)
  • How much they have given so far
  • Profession, blood group, date of birth
  • City — chosen from a list, so spellings stay the same

The old Excel register can be brought in from Donors → Import Excel, so nobody has to type hundreds of names by hand.

Office — receipts

Print, send, or cancel — with a trail

رسید چھاپیں، بھیجیں، یا منسوخ کریں

  1. Open the receipt. The Print Receipt button is at the top.
  2. You can also send the same receipt to the donor on WhatsApp.
  3. If a receipt was written by mistake, use Void this receipt. The line is not deleted. It stays, with the reason and the name of the person who cancelled it.
  4. Only the Super Admin, or a person the Super Admin has allowed, can void a receipt. Ordinary staff cannot.

Office — WhatsApp

A message to many donors

بہت سے معطین کو پیغام

We do not yet have the official WhatsApp bulk service. Until then the office can still send a proper message, one donor at a time, without typing each number.

  1. Open WhatsApp in the menu.
  2. Write one message. Put the person’s name in automatically.
  3. Choose who should receive it: everyone, monthly members, or members who have not given this month.
  4. Press send. WhatsApp opens with that donor and the text already filled. You tap Send. Then the next donor.

Office — majalis calendar

English date and Islamic date together

ہجری اور عیسوی کیلنڈر

Staff and volunteers open Calendar. They see upcoming gatherings — Ayyam-e-Fatimiyya, milad, majalis from 1 Muharram to 8 Rabi al-Awwal, and any date you add.

  1. A donor offers to sponsor a date, and writes how much they can give.
  2. The office sees the request on the same calendar screen.
  3. Approve or reject it.
  4. Then send them a WhatsApp: approved or not, the gathering, the date, and the amount.

Office — who may do what

Five seats in the office

کون کیا کر سکتا ہے

Each seat has two buttons: View (look only) and Edit (change). More seats can be added later.

Super Admin

Full control, including cancelling a receipt.

Admin

Runs the office day to day. Cannot cancel a receipt unless allowed.

Accounts

Sees every rupee. Does not change users.

Manager

Records collection for their team.

Trustee

Look only — for monitoring. Nothing can be changed by mistake.

Assets

Each item has a department and a person in charge — staff or a volunteer.

Donors — their own screen

A donor can use a phone, a tablet, or a computer

معطی گھر سے بھی دیکھ سکتا ہے

They sign in with their mobile number and a PIN. The page is wide on a computer. On a phone there are four buttons at the bottom.

  • Home — their name, this month’s giving, lifetime total
  • Pay — choose a head, write the amount, choose how they paid, attach a photo of the slip if they wish
  • Calendar — upcoming majalis, English and Islamic dates
  • Ledger — their own receipts, to look back

Donors — pay and sponsor

Giving from home, and booking a majlis

گھر سے عطیہ، اور مجلس کی سپانسرشپ

  1. On Pay they pick the collection head and the amount. They do not fill bank names or slip numbers — that is office work.
  2. They can attach a photo of the transfer. The office then checks it.
  3. On Calendar they tap a gathering they wish to sponsor.
  4. They write: “I can give this much.” The trust sees it, accepts or declines, and messages them on WhatsApp with the date and the amount.
